**Hold Right Option to talk.** Let go and the text lands at your cursor; double-tap to lock it on.

- **Terminals and Claude get typed into, not pasted** — a big paste becomes an attachment there.
- **Music pauses only when the mic can hear it** — speakers yes, headphones no.
- **Long dictations transcribe while you talk**, in 40-second windows, so letting go is near-instant.
- **Nothing gets thrown away** — Escape keeps the take, and a crash leaves it in History.
- **Recordings are kept to a size you set** — 5 GB, about 43 hours, oldest dropped first.
- **The flow bar is Wispr Flow's**, and it answers speech, not the room.
- **Your recordings stay yours** — enough of them can teach it your voice and filter other people out.
