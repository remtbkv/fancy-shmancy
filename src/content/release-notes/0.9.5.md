## Fancy Shmancy

**Hold Right Option, talk, let go — the text lands where your cursor is.** Double-tap it to lock recording on, tap again to send.

- **Terminals and Claude get it typed in, not pasted** — a single big paste becomes an attachment there, so listed apps receive it as text instead.
- **Your music only pauses when the mic can actually hear it** — speakers yes, headphones no.
- **Long dictations don't wait for you to stop** — they're transcribed in 40-second windows while you're still talking, so letting go is nearly instant.
- **Nothing you said gets thrown away** — Escape keeps the recording, and if the app dies mid-sentence the audio is still there in History next launch.
- **Recordings are kept until they hit a size you choose** — 5 GB by default, about 43 hours, oldest dropped first; never deleted for being old.
- **The flow bar is Wispr Flow's**, rebuilt from frame-by-frame measurements of theirs, and it answers speech rather than the room around you.
- **The recordings it stores are yours to use** — once there are enough, they can teach it your voice so it ignores other people talking near you, or filters them out of the transcript.
